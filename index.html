    How I work
    
        
    
          I work mostly through messages and email. Every decision is written down, and you can reply when it suits you.
        
    
        
    
          Video calls are fine when something is easier to explain face to face.
        
    
      
    

      
      
        
    1. 
          1
          
      
            
      Tell me what you need
      
            
      Send a message or email with a few lines about your business and the website or app you have in mind. Rough ideas are fine.
      
          
      
        
      2. 
        
    2. 
          2
          
      
            
      A free mock-up, if you need one Free
      
            
      If you don't have a design in mind, I make a mock-up so you can see the direction before paying anything.

    3. 
          3

      4. 
        
    4. 
          4
          
      
            
      Estimate and price range
      
            
      You get a time estimate and a price range for the agreed scope. Work starts once you approve it.

    5. 
          5
          
      
            
      Building, with regular check-ins
      
            
      On longer projects we catch up every 3 to 7 days. I show what's done, and we make the changes you need along the way, not all at the end.

    6. 
          6

      You review the final version, I set up hosting and the domain and put it live. Six months of support for bugs and minor style fixes is included.

    Questions clients ask
    
      
    
        
    
          Do I need to set up hosting and a domain myself?
          
    
            
    No. You create the accounts in your own name, so you always own them, and I handle the rest: hosting setup, domain, email, SSL and deployment. You get the finished website, ready to use.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          What support do I get after launch?
          
    
            
    Six months of support is included. It covers bug fixes and minor style changes, like adjusting colours, spacing or text.
    
            
    New features, new pages or bigger design changes are quoted separately before I start.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          Can I update the website myself?
          
    
            
    Yes. I set up an admin area where you can change text, photos and posts without touching any code, and show you how to use it.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          How long does a project take?
          
    
            
    It depends on the size of the project. Once I know what you need, you get a timeline together with the quote, so you know the launch date before we start.
